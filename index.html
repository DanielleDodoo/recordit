QUICK GUIDE

How to use recordit

Record

  1. New recording, then Start.
  2. Tap ★ Highlight when something matters. Tap ✎ Note to jot a thought.
  3. Finish & save.

On your phone

  1. Open the recording, then Send to laptop.
  2. Choose Drive, then recordit inbox, then Upload.
  3. Your laptop makes the transcript, with your highlights and notes in place.

A YouTube video or podcast (on the laptop)

  1. New recording. Record from: Browser tab / computer audio.
  2. Start, pick the video's tab, tick Share tab audio.
  3. Play it. Highlight and note as it goes.

After recording

  • Your highlights are under Bookmarks, your notes under Comments.
  • Find words searches what was said.
  • Name speakers puts names to the voices.
  • Transcribe audio again makes a new transcript and keeps the old one.

Keep everything safe

  • ⚙ Storage & offline setup, then tick Back up and sync with my other laptops.
